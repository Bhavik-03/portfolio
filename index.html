How it works

The client captures audio on a push-to-talk key and posts it to a local FastAPI server, optionally with a screenshot when the Look key is used. faster-whisper transcribes on-GPU, Gemini reasons over the text and decides whether to call one of 13 tools — web search, notes, reminders, a concept tracker, or a quiz grader — within a three-round tool budget and a staged 15-second deadline with a tool-free fallback. Piper synthesizes the reply locally and the answer audio goes back to the client, which also polls every 10 seconds and speaks reminders when they fall due. A short-term session keeps follow-ups like "tell me more" in context, and a SQLite FTS5 store backs notes, reminders and logged concepts.

Engineering decisions

Capped the agent at a three-round tool budget with a staged 15-second deadline and a tool-free fallback, so a slow or failing tool degrades into a plain answer instead of a timeout.
Kept speech-to-text and text-to-speech local on a 4 GB RTX 3050 — audio never leaves the machine, screenshots are shrunk and never written to disk, and only question text and tool results reach the API.
Froze an evaluation suite before iterating, so each version is scored on the same cases and regressions surface before release.

How it works

Shallow-UWnet, a 219K-parameter CNN, is fine-tuned on 890 paired UIEB images with an L1 + VGG16 perceptual loss, raising PSNR from 18.07 to 19.20 dB and SSIM from 0.7753 to 0.8296 — beating the authors' own pretrained weights on 67 of 89 test images. An exact banded-inference scheme processes full-resolution photo and video frames in overlapping bands, holding peak VRAM at 3.6 GB from 4K to 8K (vs. 7.1 GB single-pass), served through a FastAPI backend and a React/TypeScript web app.

Engineering decisions

Ran a baseline-first evaluation that caught the authors' pretrained weights underperforming raw input due to domain shift — before trusting any fine-tuning result.
Built an exact banded-inference scheme rather than downscaling, holding peak VRAM at 3.6 GB from 4K to 8K frames while producing results identical to a single pass.
Backed the project with 45 reproducible CPU-only tests, seeded and checkpointed experiment tracking, and SHA-256-verified checkpoint loading.
